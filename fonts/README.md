# Fonts

- **Fredoka** SemiBold (600): display face for hooks, titles, numbers and the wordmark.
- **Nunito** SemiBold, Bold and ExtraBold (600, 700, 800): body, tips, tags and captions.

Both are by their respective designers on Google Fonts and licensed under the **SIL Open Font License 1.1**. These Latin-subset WOFF files come from the `@fontsource/fredoka@5.3.0` and `@fontsource/nunito@5.3.0` npm packages. The licence allows bundling and redistribution, including commercial use.
