# Tunas Imunisasi (crayon picture book test, 40 s)

Style: Crayon Picture Book (Lemo-Opuscar `crayon-book`). Audience: children 2 to 5 years old, watched with a parent. Language: Indonesian. Voice: placeholder (espeak-ng, pitched) until the ElevenLabs voice is ready. Purpose: a style comparison against the puppet test in `tools/kartun.py`.

Content rules (TemanTumbuh CLAUDE.md): this is a social story, so it is honest. It tells the child the shot hurts for a moment and crying is allowed. It makes no promises. The only health claim is that imunisasi helps the body fight germs (Kemenkes / WHO). There is no red cross on the clinic, no needle close-up, and Mama is the hero.

## 1. Three structures

1. **A journey drawn live.** The road and the puskesmas draw themselves just ahead of Tunas and Mama. The turn is the shot. The ending is a wash revealing what the shot gave him.
2. **A colouring-book page.** Every outline is on the page from frame one, and each step of the visit colours one area (road, door, chair, plaster). It is clear, but static, and the turn has no physical moment.
3. **Before and after across a page turn.** Page one shows Tunas worried at home; page two shows him at the puskesmas, then brave. It is good for time jumps, but at 40 s the turn eats a beat we need for the feeling.

**Chosen: 1.** A social story is a walk through an event in order, so a journey that draws itself ahead of the child is literally "this is what will happen next". The wax-resist reveal gives the abstract idea ("imunisasi protects you") a picture a 3-year-old can hold: a shield that was there all along, now visible.

## 2. Logline and arc

Tunas is a little nervous about his imunisasi, but with Mama's hand to hold he gets through a short sting, and a wash of colour shows the shield his body now has.

- **Setup:** Tunas and Mama walk to the puskesmas, and the road draws itself ahead of them.
- **Turn:** close on Tunas, wobbling with nerves. Mama takes his hand, there is a beat of silence, then "cus" and a plaster.
- **Ending:** a blue wash sweeps the page and reveals a white-wax shield around Tunas. Mama hugs him.

## 3. Benchmark

A gentle hand-drawn picture-book animation (Briggs-era TV specials), plus preschool social-story videos.
- **Learn from it:** still backgrounds with living figures, music-led pacing, few words, long holds.
- **Don't take:** any characters, songs, designs or phrases.

## 6. Cue map

90 BPM, 4/4 (one bar = 2.667 s).
- **Instruments:** nylon pluck (Karplus-Strong), music box, glockenspiel, woodblock, soft shaker.
- **Bars 1-4 (0-10.7 s):** pluck lilt, walking on the beat; glock notes as the door and windows draw on.
- **Bars 5-7 (10.7-18.7 s):** thinner, pluck only, lower register.
- **Bar 8 (18.7-21.3 s):** music box enters with Mama's hand.
- **22.0-22.8 s:** silence; the woodblock "cus" lands at 22.8.
- **27.0-34.5 s:** wash; glock arpeggio rising; the shield reveals on the downbeat at 29.3.
- **34.5-40 s:** the music box theme in full, then winding down with a pitch droop at 39.

## 8. Subtitles and titles

The book's printed words: a handwriting face (Patrick Hand if it can be fetched, otherwise the closest OFL font available offline), about 54 px, in the outline colour (dark brown), bottom centre. They are written on letter by letter on a blank-paper knock-out. Each is held for at least max(1.8 s, the speech + 0.6 s).
