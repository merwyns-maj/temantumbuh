# TemanTumbuh: What Indonesian parenting content earns likes, shares, saves and comments

Research date: 17 October 2026. Scope: Indonesia, TikTok first, with Instagram and Threads as secondary signals.

## How to read this report

- **Confidence labels.** Every claim carries a label. **High** means a primary or reputable source, or several sources agree. **Medium** means one decent secondary source, or a strong pattern seen across many examples. **Low** means a vendor blog, a single anecdote, or my own inference.
- **Missing engagement numbers.** TikTok, Instagram, Threads and X block automated reading (robots-disallowed). I could not open posts to read like, save or share counts. The engagement numbers below come only from news articles, third-party trackers or published studies, and I name the source each time. Where a hook is quoted without a number, the number was not visible. I invented no numbers, handles or quotes.
- **"Inferred."** This marks my own reasoning from the evidence. It is not something a source states.

## 1) Top 10 takeaways: the rules we should adopt

1. **Every post does one job: get saved, get sent, or get comments. Choose the job before writing the hook.**
   - Checklists, per-usia tables and "jangan beli" lists get saved.
   - Posts that say something the viewer wants to tell a partner, a grandparent or the pengasuh get sent.
   - Open questions that invite people's own stories get comments.
   - On Instagram, carousels earn the most saves at every account size (Socialinsider, 35M posts, high). TikTok's ranking now weights saves and shares above likes (Sprout Social 2026, medium).

2. **Be the "proxy messenger." This is the strongest share trigger in Indonesian parenting.**
   - Wives tag husbands with "Nih, Pah, baca" because a post can say what is hard to say out loud (Mojok, medium).
   - Family WhatsApp groups avoid direct confrontation to keep the peace (indojurnal study on family WA groups, medium).
   - So write posts that someone can forward instead of starting an argument. Use no-blame framing: "Bukan salah Nenek, ilmunya aja yang udah update."

3. **Open on the moment the parent is living through, not on the topic.**
   - The highest-performing hook we could verify is "Udah sampai sejauh mana GTM nya buibu?" from @zahrairs, with 8M+ views (Liputan6, high).
   - It names a shared struggle and invites a story. It is not a topic label like "Tips mengatasi GTM".

4. **Myth vs fakta and "jangan beli dulu" are our best faceless formats. Put a source on a slide every time.**
   - Doctor accounts built their reach on these. @dokterapin is known for "mitos vs fakta"; @bicaraasikecil posts "10 barang yg gaperlu dibeli" and "Jangan dimarahin!" (verified captions; no numbers visible).
   - The "bau tangan" myth was national news in June 2026 after Alyssa Daguise rejected it (RCTI+/Sindonews, high).

5. **Ayah humour works when the dad is the joke and the mom is the hero.**
   - Self-deprecating dad content that ends by crediting istri gets moms tagging husbands.
   - Content where the dad complains turns the comments against him. When @suss.an thanked her husband for night shifts (~250K plays), mothers flooded the comments venting about their own husbands who "masih tetep ngorok" (Merdeka, medium).
   - Use illustrations, not photos. Fowl Language Comics is a proven model for faceless dad humour (inferred, medium).

6. **Hook onto news and calendar moments while they are fresh.** Live pegs right now:
   - A Grade 3 pupil died choking on a kelengkeng from the MBG school meal in Lampung on 22 Sept 2026 (high).
   - SPMB 2026 bans calistung tests for SD entry under Permendikdasmen 3/2025 (high).
   - The government cites a 25.8% "fatherless" figure, published 17 Sept 2026 (high).
   - A postpartum case in Malang was in the news on 14 Oct 2026 (medium).
   - Rainy season is October to March. Hari Ayah Nasional is 12 Nov and Hari Ibu is 22 Dec.

7. **Calm parents down; don't scare them.**
   - A UGM discourse study found @parentalk.id-style "ideal parenting standards" produce anxiety (medium).
   - Gentle parenting is giving way to "batasan yang disertai empati" and less performance (Publisita/Viva, medium).
   - Use "versi realistis", "ternyata normal" and "kamu udah ngelakuin ini kok" angles. They get shared because they relieve guilt.

8. **Carousel craft: a hook-only first slide, a payoff on slide 2, 6 to 9 slides, and a save/send prompt plus the source on the last slide.**
   - Photo mode is reported to rank on swipe-through (Sprout, medium).
   - Video gets about 5x the reach and 6x the interactions of carousels (Metricool 2026, 2.3M posts, high). Carousels win on saves and shares, not raw views.
   - Carousel volume grew about 140% year on year, so competition is rising.

9. **Treat health content as high-risk.**
   - Keep it sourced (IDAI, Kemenkes, WHO), human-reviewed and free of cure claims.
   - On 14 July 2026 TikTok announced account-level detection of AI-generated spam, with medical content named as a priority topic (Search Engine Journal, high).
   - Label any realistic AI imagery and disclose affiliate content.
   - Never promote infant formula, follow-on formula or other breast-milk substitutes. PP 28/2024 Pasal 33 bans social-media and influencer promotion of them (Katadata, high).

10. **Build trust first and keep affiliate to about 20%.** The product should appear as a tool inside a useful post ("alat MPASI yang kepake vs nggak"), not as an ad.
    - 70% of Indonesian mums buy through affiliate links and 8 in 10 rely on peer recommendations (theAsianparent Digital Mum Survey, medium; the survey year is labelled inconsistently as 2024/2025).
    - TikTok affiliate requires 1,000+ followers, age 18+ and a post within the last 28 days (Fortune IDN 2024, medium).

**Operating defaults**
- Post at 19:00 to 21:00 WIB. Also test 12:00 to 13:00, the nap window.
- End the caption with a question. Questions get about 26% more comments (Metricool).
- Use 3 to 5 specific hashtags and put the search keyword in the caption and on slide 1.

### Steady (reliable saves, lower virality)
- Tidur bayi and wake windows
- Tummy time and motor milestones (@grazia_family, @deasilistiani)
- Play ideas at home
- Book lists by age
- Toilet training
- Tantrum basics
- Imunisasi schedules

Confidence: medium. All are evergreen and searchable, but heavily covered.

### Saturated (many copies; you need a sharper angle to stand out)
- "Tanda bayi siap MPASI". Every portal (Orami, Fimela, Popmama) and many doctors cover it.
- Generic "5 tips stimulasi otak".
- Basic MPASI recipes.
- Gentle parenting 101.
- Generic "jangan marahin anak".

Confidence: medium (inferred from search-result density).

### Risky (high comments, but brand or policy danger)

| Topic | Risk | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Mertua / ipar conflict** | Comment wars; alienates the grandparents who also forward our posts | Frame as "kalimat sopan", never as villains. Debates exist (e.g. @ahmadrisyad "Bolehkah mertua ikut campur…"; Kompas Aug 2025 viral mertua case). |
| **Mom-shaming topics** (ASI vs sufor, caesar vs normal, berat badan anak) | Reported 7 in 10 mothers experience mom-shaming (Kompas 2023 headline; 72.65% survey headline) | Never judge a choice. Defend the parent instead. Low to medium confidence on the stats: articles not readable. |
| **Biaya 50:50 / gender wars** | Polarising; drags the brand into marriage fights | Use for money-planning posts only. Don't take sides. |
| **"VOC parenting" vs gentle parenting** | Facebook and comment fights (catatanbundasaladin 2025) | Take the "batas + empati" middle ground. |
| **Formula / susu pertumbuhan** | Illegal for influencers to promote breast-milk substitutes and follow-on formula (PP 28/2024 Pasal 33) | Never affiliate. Never mention brands. |
| **Medical treatment, drugs, vaccines** | Misinformation rules; TikTok AI-spam detection on medical topics (Jul 2026) | Point to a doctor. Cite IDAI. No dosing. |
| **Showing children / pranks** | Backlash (detikHealth Aug 2024 TikToker teasing case); child-privacy pressure under PP Tunas | Faceless and illustrated only. |

## 3) Formats and when each wins

| Format | Wins on | When to use | Notes / evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Checklist / per-usia guide** | Saves | MPASI, milestones, siap sekolah, food-cutting chart | Carousels lead saves on IG (Socialinsider, high). Step-by-step "always performs well" (formula-brand managers, Frontiers 2026, medium). |
| **Mitos vs fakta** | Shares + comments | Elder-generation myths, viral trend checks | The shareability comes from proxy-forwarding. Always include the source and a "bukan salah siapa-siapa" line. (Medium) |
| **"Jangan beli dulu" / kepake vs nggak** | Saves + shares + affiliate trust | Baby gear, MPASI tools, toys | Verified recurring format on doctor and mom accounts. (Medium) |
| **Relatable humour / POV** | Shares (tags) + comments | Seri Ayah Baru, newborn chaos, istri "nggak apa-apa" | POV remains a top 2026 format for emotional closeness (detik, May 2026, low to medium). |
| **Sebelum / sesudah** | Shares | Ayah before/after, playroom before/after rotation | Meme-native; fast swipe-through. (Inferred, medium) |
| **Story / confession (curhat)** | Comments | Threads-style, first-person "aku dulu…" | Threads curhat posts become news stories (detik Jun 2026). Requires a persona voice; for us, use a "kata pembaca" or composite story, clearly framed. (Medium) |
| **Comparison / "boleh vs jangan"** | Saves + comments | Tantrum, screen time, MPASI methods | Dilemma hooks like "Harus marah, atau diajak ngobrol?" (@rensia_sanvira). (Medium) |
| **Red flag / "kapan ke dokter"** | Saves + shares | Speech delay, fever, choking signs | High value but high risk. Source every point. (Medium) |
| **Question hook ("ada yg sama?")** | Comments | GTM, sleep, toddler phases | Question posts get +26.19% comments (Metricool, high). |
| **Listicle (5 hal…)** | Saves | Play ideas, books | Weakest when the title is generic. Add a situation: "buat masak 20 menit tanpa diganggu". (Inferred, medium) |

**Carousel vs short video.** Video still gets far more reach on TikTok (5x reach, 6x interactions; Metricool 2026, high). Carousels are our production advantage and earn saves and shares. Plan to recut the best carousels as 10 to 15 second text-on-screen videos once we have winners (inferred).

### 4b. Patterns distilled

These patterns are inferred from 4a. Medium confidence.

1. **Shared struggle + question:** "Udah sampai mana [masalah] kamu?" / "Ada yang sama?"
2. **Common belief, turned into a question:** "Jangan kasih HP biar nggak tantrum?!"
3. **Counter-instinct command:** "Anak lempar barang? Jangan dimarahin dulu."
4. **"Jangan beli dulu":** "Barang ini udah nggak perlu dibeli."
5. **Worry quote + "belum tentu":** "'Udah 6 bulan tapi lehernya belum tegak?' Belum tentu harus nunda."
6. **Dilemma A or B:** "Harus marah, atau diajak ngobrol?"
7. **Viral trend check:** "Lagi rame [X]. Boleh ditiru nggak?"
8. **Kebiasaan sepele:** "Hal kecil yang kamu lakuin tiap hari ternyata…"
9. **Gratitude or complaint about a partner:** this pattern triggers tagging and comparison.
10. **Local price or number:** "Rp20 ribu", "Jawa Timur 2026", "2 jam sehari".

## 5) Share, save and comment triggers

### What makes Indonesian parents send or tag

| Trigger | Mechanism | Evidence | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Proxy message to partner** | Saying what's hard to say out loud ("Nih, Pah, baca") | Mojok 2023 | Medium |
| **Polite correction of elders** | Forwarding a myth-buster to the family WA group instead of arguing face to face; harmony matters more than winning | Family WA group study (indojurnal); Alyssa Daguise coverage | Medium |
| **Safety for whoever is caring for the child** | "Kirim ke yang jagain anakmu": nenek, pengasuh, daycare | Inferred from choking news + household structures | Medium (inferred) |
| **Identity / relatability humour** | "Ini aku banget" sends to a friend or partner | @musdalifahbasrii comedic mom; dad humour | Medium |
| **Practical value + money** | Saves money or time; "anti-mubazir" | Verified "jangan beli" posts | Medium |
| **Reassurance** | "Ternyata normal" relieves guilt and gets sent to anxious friends | Anxiety-production critique of @parentalk.id (UGM) | Medium (inferred) |
| **Peer trust** | 8 in 10 mums rely on peer recommendations | theAsianparent Digital Mum Survey | Medium |

**Platform mechanic.** Instagram's Mosseri named the top three signals as "watch time, likes and sends" and pointed to sends per reach (Social Media Today, Jan 2025, high). On TikTok, shares and saves "often outweigh likes" (Sprout 2026, medium).

### What triggers saves
- Per-usia tables
- Checklists, including shopping lists
- Ready-made phrases to say to the child, partner or relatives
- Menus with prices
- "Kapan ke dokter" red flags
- Toy and book lists by age

Carousels lead saves on Instagram (high). The "Simpan" prompt is already standard on doctor accounts (verified caption, @dr.indratarigan).

### What makes comment sections explode

**Healthy (aim for these)**
- Story-collection questions. The @zahrairs comments were people listing their own GTM tricks (high).
- A or B dilemmas.
- "Ada yang sama?"
- "Usia berapa anakmu mulai…?"

**Risky (avoid or neutralise)**
- Mertua wars
- ASI vs sufor
- Caesar vs normal
- Biaya 50:50
- VOC vs gentle parenting
- Husband-bashing
- Kids being teased on camera

These reliably generate volume, but they pull the brand into conflict. They also risk reports and lost goodwill with the grandparents who forward our posts (medium). Mom-shaming is widespread: headlines report about 70 to 88% of mothers have experienced it (Kompas 2023, Tempo; low to medium, article bodies not readable).

## 7) Platform mechanics 2026

### TikTok ranking signals

| Signal | Status in 2026 | Confidence / source |
|---|---|---|
| **Saves and shares** | Weighted above likes ("the hierarchy… has shifted in 2026") | Medium (Sprout Social, Feb/Mar 2026) |
| **Completion / qualified views** | Views over 5 seconds count; low completion can stall a post around 200 views | Medium (Sprout) |
| **Photo mode swipe-through rate** | Photo mode reportedly ranks on swipe-through; swiping to the last slide signals completion | Medium-low (Sprout). The "5x reach" and "70% STR" claims from Reelbase are unsourced vendor claims (low). |
| **Search value** | On-screen text, captions and auto-captions matched to search queries; relevance beats recency, so posts can perform for weeks | Medium (Sprout) |
| **Comments** | Questions lift comments by 26.19% | High (Metricool, 2.3M posts) |
| **Hashtags** | 1+ hashtag: about 5% more views, about 9% more interactions; hashtag traffic up 114% year on year | High (Metricool) |
| **Format reach** | Video gets 5x reach and 6x interactions vs images/carousels; carousel volume up about 140% year on year | High (Metricool) |
| **Lifecycle** | Posts get 96% of their reach and 98% of interactions within 10 days | High (Metricool) |
| **Overall decline** | Views down 31%, reach down 29% year on year (Jan-Feb 2026 vs 2025) because of saturation | High (Metricool) |
| **Shares per post benchmark** | 1-5K follower pages average about 40 shares/post; 10-50K average about 120 (2025) | High (Socialinsider TikTok, 2M videos) |

### Rules: AI, low-quality and commercial content (2025-2026)

- **AI labelling.** A label is required when AI is used to realistically depict people or scenes. Stylised art, generic text-to-speech and small edits are exempt. Labelled AI content stays eligible for the For You feed. (Medium; TikTok text as quoted by Storika and Fastlane.)
  - **For us:** flat illustrations and text slides don't need a label. A photorealistic AI baby or parent does.
- **AI content slider.** Since November 2025, viewers can set "see less AI" under Manage Topics. TikTok has labelled 1.3B+ videos as AI using C2PA and invisible watermarks. (High; TikTok Newsroom, 19 Nov 2025.)
  - **Implication:** anything that looks AI-made may reach fewer people, so keep the art distinctive and human.
- **AI spam crackdown.** On 14 July 2026 TikTok began testing account-level detection of accounts "dedicated to posting AI-generated spam". The first named topics are politics and current events, financial advice and **medical content**. (High; Search Engine Journal.)
  - **For us:** health-adjacent parenting posts must be original, sourced and clearly human-edited. Avoid volume-posting templated health claims.
- **"Low-interaction" content.** I could not find an official 2026 TikTok rule using that phrase.
  - What exists: the For You feed excludes unoriginal reposts, "extremely short clips", and "false incentives for engaging" such as like-for-like (FYF standards, 2023 text; reorganised in Sept 2025).
  - TikTok's Community Guidelines version "2026H2" took effect on 24 Sept 2026. Its main change is that undisclosed commercial content may now be down-ranked or auto-labelled instead of being made automatically ineligible. (Medium; Storika, Fastlane.)
  - Avoid "like kalau setuju" and "komen 1 kalau…" bait. Soft prompts like "simpan buat nanti" or "kirim ke pasanganmu" are normal practice; I found no source saying they are penalised (low to medium).
- **Commercial disclosure.** Required when promoting your own product or posting branded or affiliate content, including when the only payment is free product. Repeated failure leads to reduced visibility, then posting restrictions. (Medium; Storika.)
- **TikTok Shop still-image rules.** Promotional videos that are mostly still images are restricted. Photo-mode carousels are reportedly evaluated differently. (Low; Moras vendor blog, US-focused; check TikTok Shop Indonesia Seller Academy before affiliate posts.)

### Indonesia context

- **Platform reach.** TikTok is the most-accessed social platform (31.8%), followed by Instagram (27.7%) and Facebook (20.4 to 29.4%; sources differ). Threads is at 1%. APJII 2026, n=8,700. (High.)
- **Daily use.** 44.9% of users spend 1 to 2 hours a day on social media. (High.)
- **Indonesian mums.** 90%+ use social media daily and Instagram is their most-used platform. Shopee leads marketplaces and TikTok Shop is rising. (Medium; theAsianparent.)

### Posting times

- **Global peak:** 18:00 to 21:00, with 20:00 the strongest (Metricool, high, global).
- **Indonesian blogs:** 19:00 to 22:00 WIB is "prime time" (romisaputra 2026, low; no data cited).
- **Our plan (inferred for parents):**
  - Primary slot: 19:00 to 21:00 WIB, after dinner and around kids' bedtime.
  - Test slot: 12:00 to 13:00 WIB, nap time and lunch break.
  - Weekends: 09:00 to 11:00.
  - Check TikTok Analytics follower-activity data after about 30 posts.

### Captions, hashtags and slides

- **Caption.**
  - The first line repeats the search keyword in natural words.
  - The next 1 to 2 lines give context.
  - End with one question.
  - Add 3 to 5 specific hashtags, e.g. #mpasi #gtm #tumbuhkembanganak #parentingindonesia plus a series tag such as #SeriMPASI.
  - Avoid #fyp spam. (Medium; Metricool recommends 1 to 5 specific hashtags.)
- **Slide count.** TikTok photo mode allows up to 35 images (postfa.st citing TikTok Help Center, medium). There is no reliable public data on the best count for TikTok.
  - On Instagram, older Socialinsider/SEJ data shows engagement above 2% when all 10 slides are used (low to medium, dated).
  - **Recommendation:** 6 to 9 slides. Slide 1 is the hook only. Slide 2 gives an immediate payoff. Slides 3 to 7 are the meat. The last slide is a recap plus source plus a save/send prompt.
- **Sizes.** 1080x1440 (3:4) works. TikTok's native size is 9:16 (1080x1920), and 4:5 is also supported.
  - Keep critical text away from the bottom ~20% (caption overlay) and the right edge (action icons). This is inferred from UI layout, so test on a phone.
  - Use one aspect ratio across all slides.

## 8) What to avoid (flops or gets penalised)

| Avoid | Why | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Clinical, textbook copy** ("Stimulasi motorik halus merupakan…") | TikTok is valued for being "concise, practical and easy-to-understand" (BBG study of 92 mothers) | Medium |

| **AI-sounding copy and imagery** | Viewers can now turn AI content down; a Bynder 2024 study found 52% disengage when they suspect AI (about articles, so a weak transfer) | Medium (slider) / low (52%) |

| **Salesy affiliate posts / product-first hooks** | Brand captions like "dukung juga dengan susu pertumbuhan…" read as ads. Metricool: video >> stills for reach. TikTok Shop limits still-image promos. | Medium |
| **Infant / follow-on formula promotion** | Illegal for influencers under PP 28/2024 Pasal 33 | High |
