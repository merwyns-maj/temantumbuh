## @omonganibu: "Ma, liat deh!" (posted about 2 Nov, saved by Merwyn 5 Nov)

- **Link:** https://vt.tiktok.com/ZSp2DmhYM/ (screen recording in the 5 Nov chat)
- **Format:** animated video, about 45 s, voice-over, flat illustrated mom and child, dark teal background. Last frame equals the first frame, so it loops.
- **Visible engagement after 3 days:** 17.4K likes, 253 comments, 3,754 saves, 3,776 shares (shares about 22% of likes, a strong send signal).
- **Structure (the part worth learning):**
  1. Scene with dialogue: child "Ma, liat deh!", mom on phone "Hmm... iya, bagus."
  2. Confession hook: "Jujur, siapa yang pernah begini?"
  3. Guilt removed before the lesson: "Bukan karena nggak sayang. Kita capek, kerjaan numpuk, HP terus bunyi."
  4. Child's inner voice: "Ceritaku nggak penting."
  5. Three numbered steps (Langkah 1/3 to 3/3), each with an exact line to say ("Tunggu ya, Mama selesaiin satu chat ini", "Wah, ceritain dong, gimana bikinnya?"), plus a 10-second countdown animation.
  6. Quotable thesis: "Dengerin hal kecilnya sekarang, biar nanti dia tetap cerita hal besarnya."
  7. CTA without blame: "Kirim ke pasangan. Kita belajar bareng."
- **Why it works:** a recognisable moment instead of a claim, guilt relief (same spirit as our "bukan salah Nenek"), the child's point of view as the emotional turn, and scripts people save.
- **What we take:** the structure only (scene, confession, child's view, 3 scripts, thesis, kirim ke pasangan). Not their topic, lines or art.
- **Our versions proposed (0 to 3):** "Udah, jangan nangis" (child hears: perasaanku salah), "Sini Mama aja, lama" (aku nggak bisa), "Malu-maluin aja!" (aku anak nakal). Needs illustrated parent and child characters in our flat style; in humour, the dad is the one who slips.
- **Also note:** our earlier animated videos (Susu nari, Tunas intro) flopped without a story. This suggests animation works when there is a scene and an emotion. Test the structure as a carousel first.
